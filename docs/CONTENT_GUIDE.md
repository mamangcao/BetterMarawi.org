# 📝 Content Writing Guide for Local Government Websites

This guide helps content contributors create clear, helpful, and accessible content for local government websites. It applies to all content on BetterMarawi.org.

---

## 🎯 Content Principles

### 1. Citizen-First Approach

- Write for your constituents, not for government officials
- Use simple, clear language
- Focus on what citizens need to know and do
- Include practical, actionable information
- Answer the question people are actually asking: "What do I need, where do I go, how much, how long?"

### 2. Accessibility & Inclusion

- Use plain language (avoid jargon and acronyms)
- Write in the language your constituents speak
- Include multiple ways to access information (online, phone, in person)
- Consider different literacy levels
- Do not rely on color, images, or PDFs alone to carry important information

### 3. Accuracy & Timeliness

- Verify all information before publishing
- Include dates and update schedules
- Provide current contact information
- Update content regularly
- If you cannot verify something, do not publish it as fact (see [Unverified Information](#-unverified-information))

---

## ✍️ Writing Guidelines

### Language and Tone

**✅ DO:**

- Use active voice: "You can apply online" not "Applications can be submitted online"
- Write in second person: "You need to bring..." not "Applicants need to bring..."
- Use simple words: "help" not "assistance"
- Be specific: "Bring 2 valid IDs" not "Bring identification"
- Keep sentences short (aim for 20 words or fewer)
- Put the most important information first
- Be respectful and welcoming, including to residents who are displaced, elderly, or unfamiliar with government processes

**❌ DON'T:**

- Use government jargon: "LGU" instead of "local government"
- Use acronyms without explanation
- Write in third person
- Use passive voice unnecessarily
- Use long, legal-sounding sentences
- Use ALL CAPS for emphasis (it is harder to read)

### Plain-Word Swaps

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| assistance | help |
| utilize | use |
| commence | start |
| terminate | end |
| prior to | before |
| in the event that | if |
| endeavor | try |
| pursuant to | under / following |
| remit payment | pay |
| the undersigned | I / we |
| applicant shall | you must |

### Before and After Examples

| ❌ Before | ✅ After |
| --- | --- |
| Applications for business permits may be submitted by applicants at the BPLO during office hours. | You can apply for a Mayor's Permit at the Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO), Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. |
| Requirements include valid identification. | Bring 2 valid IDs (for example, a passport and a driver's license). |
| The LGU provides medical assistance to qualified indigent residents. | The city government gives free medical help to residents who qualify. |

## 🔤 Acronyms and Official Names

- Spell out the full name the first time, then add the acronym in parentheses: "City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)".
- After the first use on a page, the acronym alone is fine.
- Prefer the plain description when it is clearer: "city health office" over "CHO".
- Use official office, program, and law names exactly as published (for example, in the Citizen's Charter).

---

## 🗂️ Page Structure

### General rules

- One clear page title (H1) that says what the page is about
- Use headings (H2, H3) in order, without skipping levels
- Start with a short summary: who this is for and what they can do
- Use short paragraphs (2 to 4 sentences)
- Use numbered lists for steps, and bullet lists for items with no order
- Use tables for fees, schedules, and comparisons, and keep them simple

### Service pages

Every service page should answer these questions, in this order:

1. **What is this service?** (one or two sentences)
2. **Who can use it?** (eligibility)
3. **What do you need to bring?** (requirements checklist, specific documents and number of copies)
4. **What are the steps?** (numbered, in order)
5. **How much does it cost?** (fees, or "Free")
6. **How long does it take?** (processing time)
7. **Where do you go?** (office name, address, floor or window)
8. **When is it open?** (days and hours, plus holidays or closures)
9. **Who can you contact?** (phone, email, office)
10. **Frequently asked questions**
11. **Last reviewed date**

## 🔢 Numbers, Dates, Money, and Time

- **Money:** use the peso sign and two decimals where needed: ₱1,500.00. Write "Free" instead of ₱0.00.
- **Dates:** write the month in words: October 19, 2026. This avoids day/month confusion.
- **Time:** use AM/PM with a space: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- **Phone numbers:** write the full number with area code, and make it a tap-to-call link: (063) 352-0117.
- **Large numbers:** use commas: 207,010.
- **Percentages and units:** write "20%" and "700 meters above sea level".
- **Population and statistics:** always state the source and year, for example "207,010 (2020 Census, PSA)".

## 🌐 Languages and Translation

The site supports English (EN), Filipino (FIL), and Meranaw (MRW).

- English is the source version. Write it first, in plain language.
- Translate meaning, not word for word. Short, simple English translates better.
- Have translations reviewed by a fluent speaker before publishing. Do not rely only on machine translation, especially for Meranaw.
- Keep names of offices, laws, and programs consistent across languages. Add a short explanation if the name is not self-explanatory.
- If a page is not yet translated, show the English version rather than a poor translation.
- Phone numbers, dates, and figures must be identical in all languages.

## 🔗 Links, Images, and Documents

### Links

- Use descriptive link text: "Read the Citizen's Charter" not "click here"
- Tell people when a link opens a PDF or an external site
- Check links regularly, and remove or fix broken ones

### Images

- Add short, useful alt text that describes what the image shows or why it matters
- Do not put important text inside an image
- Use only photos and graphics you have the right to use, and credit the source when required
- Avoid photos of identifiable people, especially children, without permission

### Documents (PDF, spreadsheets)

- Put the key information on the web page itself, not only in a file
- Name files clearly: `annual-budget-2026.pdf`, not `final_v2.pdf`
- Include the title, year, and issuing office in the document
- Offer data tables as CSV or JSON where possible (see Open Data)

## 📞 Contact Information

Every service page and office listing should include, where available:

- Office name and head (if public)
- Physical address and how to find the office
- Phone number and email
- Office hours
- Last verified date

Do not publish personal mobile numbers or home addresses of officials or staff unless they are official public contact lines.

---

## 🔒 Privacy and Sensitive Content

- Do not publish personal data of residents (names with addresses, ID numbers, health or case details)
- Follow the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) and your office's data privacy policy
- Do not publish case files, complaints, or beneficiary lists unless they are required to be public and have been cleared for release
- Describe sensitive topics (disasters, displacement, health) factually and with respect
- Use respectful, inclusive language when referring to Persons with Disabilities, senior citizens, women, and displaced families

---

## ⚠️ Unverified Information

- Never publish a figure, name, deadline, or phone number you have not checked against an official source
- Record the source and the date you verified it in `docs/data-sources.md`
- If something is a placeholder or sample, label it clearly, or leave it out
- When rules or fees change, update the page the same day and update the "last reviewed" date

---

## 📅 Update Schedule

| Content type | Review |
| --- | --- |
| Emergency hotlines and contact numbers | Every month |
| Service requirements, fees, and steps | Every quarter, or when the Citizen's Charter changes |
| Officials and department heads | After every election or appointment |
| Budget, FDP, and transparency documents | Quarterly and annually, following DILG and COA schedules |
| Statistics and profiles | When new official data is released |
| Announcements and events | Remove or archive once they are over |

Every page that people depend on should show a **"Last updated"** date.

---

## ✅ Pre-Publish Checklist

Before you publish or submit content, confirm:

- [ ] I wrote for citizens, using plain language and active voice
- [ ] I addressed the reader as "you"
- [ ] I explained every acronym on first use
- [ ] Facts, figures, fees, and names are verified, with sources recorded
- [ ] Phone numbers, addresses, and hours are current
- [ ] Dates, times, and money follow the formats above
- [ ] Steps are numbered, and requirements are specific
- [ ] Headings are in order, and links say where they go
- [ ] Images have alt text, and no important text is inside an image
- [ ] No private personal data is included
- [ ] The page has a "Last updated" date
- [ ] Translations (FIL, MRW) are reviewed, or the page clearly falls back to English
- [ ] Someone else has read it and understood it on the first try

---

## 🧪 The Final Test

Read your content aloud to someone who has never used the service. If they can tell you what to bring, where to go, how much it costs, and what happens next, the content is ready.
